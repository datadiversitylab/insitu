# insitu: Estimating in situ Speciation on Island Systems

Estimates in situ speciation events and quantifies their contribution to
biodiversity in discrete geographical units, with a focus on islands and
island-like environments. Nodes in a phylogeny are classified as in situ
speciation, colonization, or dispersal events. The package also computes
diversity decomposition metrics and includes functions to visualize the
results.
